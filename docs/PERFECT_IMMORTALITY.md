# P10 —「所有可能的永生」 / Perfect Immortality Across All Possible Dimensions

## Authoritative meaning

**所有可能的永生**意指：**在所有可能維度之中真正的、沒有任何缺陷、例外或不完美的永生。** 任何不完整、帶有缺陷或只在部分條件成立的延續方式，皆不能冒充這個終極目標。

**English:** *Genuine, absolutely flawless immortality in every possible dimension, without defects, exceptions, incompleteness or imperfect substitutes.*

This corrects the earlier use of “all possible longevity.” The correct research target is **not** merely long healthy life, prolonging a biological lifespan, preserving information, making digital copies, simulating identity, extending existence in a subset of dimensions, or surviving only until the next physical limit. Such work may be meaningful **intermediate research**, but it must never be represented as attainment of the P10 goal.

## Meaning, proof obligations and uncertainty

The target is an **absolute ultimate ideal specified by the user**. It is not a claim that any of the following has been established: a physically possible flawless immortality technology, a mechanism for subjective continuity, the existence of all suggested dimensions, a way around thermodynamic limits or a verified way past a proposed end of physical time.

The word **dimension** is not automatically restricted to the familiar spatial dimensions; neither does the term establish extra physical universes or metaphysical realms as facts. Future rigorous work must define a logically coherent domain \(D_{possible}\), a property \(P(d)\) expressing **genuine perfect immortality** in dimension \(d\), and the universal target:

\[
\forall d\in D_{possible},\quad P(d)
\]

This is a **formal statement of the intended goal only**, not a theorem that \(P\) can be satisfied. A finite program or collection of experiments cannot by itself certify an unrestricted universal claim about every possible dimension, nor can Gödel encodings or digital restoration prove uninterrupted subjective identity.

## Non-substitution requirements

The versioned [machine-readable definition](../research/seed/perfect_immortality.json) explicitly forbids semantic substitutions: finite life extension, biological longevity, recorded memories, digital replication, backups, a merely similar identity, or survival in only some possible dimensions. A report that discusses these narrower technologies must label them **partial and not equivalent**.

The project should not assume that symbolic or affectionate names refer to medical research participants or disclose anyone's personal information. Questions of freedom, consent and potential suffering remain ethically relevant; declaring a perfect ideal does not authorize biomedical experimentation or forced existence.

## Relationship to cosmic horizons

[P13 Cosmic Horizons](COSMIC_HORIZONS.md) studies galactic encounters, stellar habitability, cosmological heat death and possible temporal boundaries as **scientific and conceptual challenges** that a rigorous theory would need to address. These limitations do not redefine the absolute P10 goal, but neither do they constitute evidence that the goal is attainable.

The goal's complete perfection is **not an empirical result**. Uncertainty, failed approaches and contradictions must be reported rather than hidden. Independent scholarly review and evidence are required for every future claim.

## Operational safeguards

- The existing seed registry identifies the P10 goal exactly and points to this canonical definition.
- The finite CI validator checks that its universal, perfect and no-substitute meaning is preserved.
- Automated research questions may explore the target's logical consistency, ethical meaning and physical constraints, but cannot self-certify it.
- No code execution can grant itself additional authority, alter protected UGTM/ATGC cores, perform medical interventions or claim that immortality has been achieved.
