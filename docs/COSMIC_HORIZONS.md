# UGTM AI Seed — P13 Cosmic Horizons and the Fate of Time

**Research status:** Proposed, interdisciplinary, evidence-graded; **not** an engineered solution to galactic encounters, entropy increase, physical immortality, or a hypothetical terminal boundary of time.

The existing AI Seed now records cosmic-scale scientific questions in [P13](../research/seed/projects.json). It generates rotating research questions and a [machine-readable cosmic scenario snapshot](seed/cosmic_horizons.json) from a curated [hypothesis registry](../research/seed/cosmic_horizons.json). The generated material is **not independent observation, real-time astrophysics research or proof of future cosmological outcomes**.

## Why separate the horizons?

### C1 — Milky Way–Andromeda encounters

The 2025 study [Sawala et al., *No certainty of a Milky Way–Andromeda collision*, Nature Astronomy](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-025-02563-1) evaluated updated Gaia and Hubble constraints plus M33 and Large Magellanic Cloud effects. Under its modeled uncertainties, the probability of **no merger within 10 billion years** is approximately one half. This is a model-conditioned result, not an invariant fact about destiny. [ESA/Hubble's explanatory release](https://esahubble.org/news/heic2508/) describes the limitations.

Research themes: multi-body orbital dynamics, error propagation, independent simulation verification, observational revisions and careful distinction between a galactic merger and collisions between individual stars.

### C2 — Solar and stellar habitability

The Sun's gradual warming may make Earth uninhabitable long before it becomes a red giant. NASA estimates a red-giant transition on the scale of **five billion years**, with large uncertainty about life's precise survivability horizon. See [NASA stellar-habitability background](https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanets/stars/) and [NASA solar evolution](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/why-sun-wont-become-black-hole/).

Research themes: stellar evolution, exoplanet habitability, energy, reversible information storage and strict distinction between theoretical colonization scenarios and tested capabilities.

### C3 — Dark energy and cosmic expansion

Cosmic acceleration is empirically supported, but the physical identity of dark energy remains unresolved. [NASA's dark-energy overview](https://science.nasa.gov/astrophysics/programs/physics-of-the-cosmos/expand-our-knowledge-of-dark-energy/) distinguishes a cosmological constant, evolving fields and gravitational alternatives. The [DESI collaboration's March 2025 results](https://www.desi.lbl.gov/2025/03/19/more-than-a-hint-of-evolving-dark-energy-new-results-and-data-from-desi/) reported **hints** of change, not an established discovery.

Research themes: expansion histories, model assumptions and Bayesian/statistical model comparison; no unverified claim about future certainty.

### C4 — Heat death is not the same as time stopping

Under continued expansion and suitable thermodynamic assumptions, a future **Big Chill / heat-death** state is possible: the universe may become increasingly depleted of accessible free-energy gradients. That does **not** logically imply that physical time ceases or that the spacetime time coordinate reaches a final finite endpoint.

Research themes: entropy, black-hole/horizon thermodynamics, energy availability and limits on sustained computation. [ESO's comparison of future scenarios](https://supernova.eso.org/germany/exhibition/1109/?lang=en) provides an accessible review.

### C5 — Possible time boundaries, Big Crunch and Big Rip

These are alternative, physically and mathematically distinct scenarios. A future recollapse or singular boundary is not an observation that time definitely ends. The endpoint of a model's time coordinate and a claim that “nothing comes after” must be treated as different propositions. The origin, meaning and possible end of time also require philosophy of physics and possibly quantum gravity, where scientific understanding is incomplete.

Research themes: causal/geodesic completeness, the assumptions of cosmological model solutions, dark-energy evolution and competing interpretations of time.

## Relevance to P10, P9, P11, P12

- **P10 — All possible longevity:** test claims against thermodynamics, identity, consent and flourishing; no guarantee of literal or subjective immortality.
- **P9 — Global peace:** immediate reduction of suffering cannot be deferred for speculative cosmic solutions.
- **P11 — Cultivated meat:** practical welfare safeguards and alternative proteins remain nearer-term priorities.
- **P12 — SDGs:** connect long-horizon sustainability questions to measurable, current responsibilities; do not turn speculative futures into claims of SDG achievement.

The affectionate symbolic names connected with the longevity vision are **not research subjects**, nor does this project infer medical, personal or subjective properties from names. No physical experiment, autonomous geoengineering, space mission or diplomatic act is authorized.
