ABSTRACT

We study a three-outcome statistical family that arises from a normalized
three-level pure-state curve. In the probability coordinate t, its classical
Fisher information is 2/[t(1-t)] and diverges at t=0 and t=1. Under the
coordinate t=sin^2(theta/2), the Fisher metric becomes the constant
2 dtheta^2 on the interior; the underlying pure-state curve is smooth and
admits an extension across the coordinate endpoints. We distinguish this
regularization of a parameter representation from removal of a physical
spacetime singularity. We also establish an exact multinomial/entropy
asymptotic relation using Stirling's formula, formulate a restricted
correspondence condition for re-encoded computations and Lorentz-related
proper-time descriptions, and describe a base-257 reversible encoding of
symbolic calculation certificates. These results motivate a bounded,
reproducible research profile for the TM-009 prototype. They do not prove
the existence of chronons, a photonized cosmological barrier, a quantum
computer, an unrestricted regularization algorithm, or a solution of the
halting problem.

KEYWORDS
Computable analysis; Fisher information; information geometry; qutrit;

coordinate regularization; universal computation.

1. INTRODUCTION AND STATUS OF THE PROPOSAL

A divergent coefficient in a model may indicate a physical pathology, an
ill-chosen parameter, a change in the support of a probability distribution,
or a breakdown of a chosen approximation. These alternatives must not be
conflated. This paper investigates an elementary but fully analyzable
information-geometric example. Its motivation is the proposed research
program called the "Photonic Singularity Universal God Turing Machine"
(TM-009), where "photonic singularity" is a project name for a possible
information-boundary phenomenon, not an established physical entity.

The term "Universal Correspondence Principle" (UCP) is used here as a
PROPOSED verification requirement: distinct admissible representations
of the same modeled process should give consistent observables when
connected by explicit, computable translations. It is not presented as an
independently established law of nature.

The main contributions of this draft are:
  (i) a transparent derivation and endpoint qualification of the
      classical Fisher information coordinate transformation;
 (ii) a pure-state quantum Fisher information calculation;
(iii) an exact special case and an asymptotic combinatorial construction
      relating multinomial counts to Shannon entropy;
 (iv) a formal distinction between exact symbolic encoding and exact
      prediction of real physical phenomena; and
  (v) a falsifiable outline for a prospective photonic time-bin experiment.

The statistical and quantum Fisher information formulae, Stirling
asymptotics, and reversible numeral encodings used here are established
mathematics. The proposed contribution is their explicitly scoped,
reproducible integration, not a claim to have discovered those results.

2. DEFINITIONS: A THREE-STATE MODEL

Let H = C^3 with orthonormal vectors |0>, |1>, |2>. For theta in [0,pi],
define the normalized real pure state

 (1) |psi(theta)> = cos^2(theta/2)|0>
                    + sin(theta)/sqrt(2)|1>
                    + sin^2(theta/2)|2>.

Its normalization follows from the identity

 cos^4(theta/2) + (sin^2(theta))/2 + sin^4(theta/2) = 1.

Measurement in the distinguished computational basis yields

 (2) p(theta) = (cos^4(theta/2), sin^2(theta)/2,
                  sin^4(theta/2)).

Set

 (3) t = sin^2(theta/2),  0 <= t <= 1.

Then p(t) = ((1-t)^2, 2t(1-t), t^2), the binomial distribution
for two Bernoulli trials. The corresponding normalized amplitudes for
0 <= t <= 1 are

 (4) a(t) = (1-t, sqrt(2t(1-t)), t).

This is a toy model of a three-state degree of freedom. No physical
Hamiltonian or optical measurement has yet been assigned to these
amplitudes. A three-time-bin implementation is conceivable, but it is
not demonstrated by equations (1)-(4) alone.

Definition 2.1. For a probability family p_k(x) with strictly positive
entries on the interior of its domain, its classical Fisher information
in the parameter x is

 (5) I_x = sum_{k=0}^2 [ (d p_k/dx)^2 / p_k ].

Definition 2.2. The base-two Shannon entropy is

 (6) H_2(p) = -sum_{k=0}^2 p_k log_2(p_k),

with 0 log_2(0) defined as 0 by continuity.

Definition 2.3. For a differentiable normalized pure-state curve,
its quantum Fisher information is

 (7) F_Q(x) = 4 ( <d_x psi|d_x psi>
                    - |<psi|d_x psi>|^2 ).

3. THEOREM: A FISHER COORDINATE SINGULARITY

Proposition 3.1. For 0<t<1, the family in (4) has

 (8) I_t = 2/[t(1-t)].

Proof. Treat p_k(t) as the binomial probabilities for two independent
Bernoulli trials. Their score is (k-2t)/[t(1-t)] for outcome k=0,1,2,
with mean k=2t and variance Var(k)=2t(1-t). Therefore

 I_t = Var(k)/[t^2(1-t)^2] = 2/[t(1-t)]. QED.

In particular, I_t -> +infinity as t -> 0+ or t -> 1-.
Nevertheless, the distribution p(t) and H_2(p(t)) have finite
continuous limits at both endpoints: H_2(p(0)) = H_2(p(1)) = 0.

Proposition 3.2. Under the reparameterization (3), for 0<theta<pi,

 (9) I_theta = I_t (dt/dtheta)^2 = 2.

Proof. We have dt/dtheta = sin(theta)/2 and
 t(1-t) = sin^2(theta)/4. Substitute into (8):

 I_theta = [2/(sin^2(theta)/4)] [sin^2(theta)/4] = 2. QED.

Interpretation. The information line element is

 (10) ds_F^2 = I_t dt^2 = 2 dtheta^2

on the open interval. Thus the divergent coefficient in the t chart
can be written as a finite constant in the theta chart. The distance
from any interior point theta_0 to theta=0 is sqrt(2)*theta_0,
which is finite. In this particular family the endpoint admits a
continuous metric completion and a smooth state-vector extension in
theta. This is a COORDINATE REGULARIZATION of the interior information
geometry, not proof that all statistical structures are regular
under every possible endpoint convention.

Important endpoint qualification: at t=0 or t=1 some outcomes have
zero probability, the common-support assumption behind standard
Fisher/Cramer-Rao regularity conditions can fail, and the formula in
(5) contains zero denominators. The constant 2 in (9) is an INTERIOR
LIMIT. Extending theta to negative values makes p(theta)=p(-theta)
near zero, causing a global sign-identifiability issue for a
measurement in the distinguished basis. The pure states, which retain
the sign of their middle amplitude, need not be identical. These
issues cannot be removed by declaring an infinite expression finite.

4. QUANTUM INFORMATION AND A DISTINCT EXPERIMENTAL QUESTION

Proposition 4.1. For (1), F_Q(theta)=2.

Proof. Differentiate (1):

 (11) |psi'(theta)> = -sin(theta)/2 |0>
                           + cos(theta)/sqrt(2) |1>
                           + sin(theta)/2 |2>.

The inner products obey
 <psi|psi'>=0 and <psi'|psi'>=1/2.
Hence (7) gives F_Q(theta)=4*(1/2)=2. QED.

The same parameter value 2 occurs in the classical measurement
and the pure-state quantum metric for this special family. It should
not be generalized to arbitrary measurements or noise channels.

If one ADDS the dynamical hypothesis theta(tau)=omega*tau, where tau
is the proper time along a laboratory clock worldline and omega is a
fixed clock angular frequency, then the chain rule yields

 (12) I_tau = F_Q(tau) = 2*omega^2

where the interior assumptions and the ideal unitary pure-state model
hold. Equation (12) is conditional on an independently specified
physical clock dynamics. The current TM-009 exact encoder uses an
independent rational parameter u, not a demonstrated physical
u(tau); it does not by itself implement (12).

5. RATIONAL PARAMETERIZATION AND EXACT SYMBOLIC ARITHMETIC

Set

 (13) u = tan(theta/2),  u >= 0 for theta in [0,pi).

For every finite u,

 (14) |psi(u)> = [ |0> + sqrt(2)*u |1> + u^2 |2> ]/(1+u^2),

 (15) p(u) = [ 1, 2u^2, u^4 ]/(1+u^2)^2.

When u is rational, all probabilities in (15) are rational. This
permits exact arithmetic with reduced integer fractions rather than
floating-point approximations. From dtheta/du=2/(1+u^2) and (9),

 (16) I_u = 8/(1+u^2)^2,

an exact rational function. The endpoint theta=pi is represented by
u=+infinity rather than any finite rational value; it requires a
separate reciprocal chart or a limiting argument.

Example 5.1. For u=1,

 (17) p=(1/4,1/2,1/4), H_2(p)=3/2 bits,
      I_u=2, I_theta=2.

For generic rational u, Shannon entropy need not be rational.
An expression involving exact rational coefficients and formal
log_2 terms is an EXACT SYMBOLIC REPRESENTATION, not a finite
numerical evaluation of the real number with infinite precision.
This distinction is central to correct use of the word "exact".

6. RELATIVISTIC PROPER-TIME CONSISTENCY

In 1+1-dimensional flat spacetime, set c=1. Consider two events
separated along an inertial machine worldline by coordinate time T
and spatial displacement x=v*T, with rational T>=0 and |v|<1.
The invariant squared proper-time interval is

 (18) tau^2 = T^2 - x^2 = T^2(1-v^2).

Under a Lorentz boost with dimensionless velocity f, |f|<1,

 (19) T' = gamma_f*T*(1-f*v),
      x' = gamma_f*T*(v-f),
      gamma_f=1/sqrt(1-f^2).

Proposition 6.1. The squared proper-time expressions agree exactly:

 (20) T'^2-x'^2
    = T^2*[(1-f*v)^2-(v-f)^2]/(1-f^2)
    = T^2(1-v^2) = tau^2.

Proof. Expand the numerator and factor (1-f^2)(1-v^2). QED.

The TM-009 exact script verifies this as an identity on supplied
rational parameters. It represents tau by a rational number when its
square has rational numerator and denominator that are perfect
squares, or by the symbolic expression sqrt(tau^2) otherwise.
This is an EXACT algebraic representation, not an infinite-precision
floating measurement, and (20) is not a test of curved-spacetime
Einstein field equations.

For T=10 and v=3/5, tau^2=64 and tau=8 exactly, independent of
f. Nothing in this argument assigns a rest frame to a photon.

7. STIRLING ASYMPTOTICS AND INFORMATION REGULARIZATION

Let n_0,n_1,n_2 be nonnegative integers with sum N. The number of
ordered strings having these occupation numbers is

 (21) W_N = N!/(n_0!n_1!n_2!).

Assume fixed rational probabilities p_0,p_1,p_2>0 that sum to one
and take N along positive multiples of their common denominator,
so that n_k=N*p_k are integers. Let

 (22) h(p) = -sum p_k ln p_k,  H_2(p)=h(p)/ln 2.

Proposition 7.1. Stirling's formula implies

 (23) lim_{N->infinity} [ln W_N]/N = h(p).

Proposition 7.2. Along the same integer subsequence,

 (24) W_N ~ exp(N*h(p)) /
            [ (2*pi*N)*sqrt(p_0*p_1*p_2) ],

and therefore the normalizer

 (25) R_N = (2*pi*N)*W_N*exp(-N*h(p))

has the finite limit

 (26) lim_{N->infinity} R_N = 1/sqrt(p_0*p_1*p_2).

Proof of 7.1 and 7.2. Apply n! ~ sqrt(2*pi*n)*(n/e)^n to (21).
The leading logarithmic term is N*h(p); the three square-root
factors in the denominator divided into sqrt(2*pi*N) leave
1/[(2*pi*N)*sqrt(p_0*p_1*p_2)]. QED.

At p=(1/4,1/2,1/4), (26) equals 4*sqrt(2). This is a
nonconstant finite asymptotic invariant of the chosen probability
family, not a cosmological singularity or an intrinsic quantum
topological invariant. If a p_k approaches zero, (24) is not
uniformly valid in that limit: the number of occupied categories
and its square-root normalizer must be reconsidered. Divergence of
(26) as p_k->0 is not by itself evidence of any physical blow-up.

8. REVERSIBLE BASE-257 GODEL ENCODING

For a finite byte string b=(b_1,...,b_m), 0<=b_i<=255, define

 (27) G(b) = (((1*257+(b_1+1))*257+(b_2+1))* ...)
            *257+(b_m+1).

Set G(empty)=1. Each encoded digit b_i+1 lies in {1,...,256};
base-257 numerals with leading sentinel 1 therefore allow unique
reconstruction on the image of G. In particular,

 (28) D(G(b))=b

for every finite byte string b, using the corresponding inverse
algorithm. This proof does not assert that D(n) succeeds for every
natural number n: arbitrary inputs may contain an inadmissible
zero digit.

To form a certificate, serialize a deterministic report containing
rational strings and formal algebraic expressions into a finite UTF-8
byte sequence, then encode it with (27). The Gödel integer can be
large, but the mapping preserves the bytes exactly.

A successful byte-roundtrip establishes data integrity relative to
the selected serialization. It does NOT establish semantic truth of
the formulas, proof of a Kleene recursion theorem, self-consciousness,
or the absence of machine-resource limits. A Z-style fixed-point
combinator can implement anonymous recursion in a programming
language, but that is distinct from a proof of the general
computability-theoretic fixed-point theorems.

9. A RESTRICTED UNIVERSAL CORRESPONDENCE REQUIREMENT

Suppose X and Y are represented state spaces, F_X and F_Y are
specified evolution maps on a common admissible interval, and
E: X -> Y is a computable translation. Define the following
verifiable CONDITIONS, not a new universal theorem:

 (29) E(F_X(x)) = F_Y(E(x)),

 (30) O_X(x) = O_Y(E(x)),

where O_X and O_Y are stated observables. At a limiting boundary,
require an explicitly chosen topology, a proof that a relevant
extension exists and is unique within the stated class, and an
effective method for approximating the observables to arbitrary
requested accuracy with a computable error bound.

For the present toy model, the map t=sin^2(theta/2) induces
Fisher metric agreement on 0<theta<pi. The maps are not globally
regular coordinate diffeomorphisms at the endpoints, and the
choice of observables matters. Lorentz covariance in section 6
holds only for the specified inertial event-pair construction.
Neither fact entails a general UCP for arbitrary spacetime or
quantum systems.

The relevant computational distinctions are:
  exact mathematical identity;
  computable real with effective approximation procedure;
  finite-precision evaluation with a demonstrated error estimate;
  empirical measurement with uncertainty;
  speculative interpretation lacking a physical model.

An exact textual expression may denote a computable real without
itself supplying a numerical error bound at each precision request.
An effective implementation of transcendental functions and
controlled approximations remains a separate engineering task.

11. WHAT WOULD CONSTITUTE A PHYSICAL TEST?

A candidate experiment could encode a controlled pure qutrit state
in three photonic time bins, calibrate the preparation parameter
theta, measure the outcome probabilities in a fixed basis, and
estimate Fisher information from repeated trials. One would test
whether the interior statistical response is consistent with
I_theta=2 after accounting for detector loss, mixedness, phase
noise, mode distinguishability, and finite-sample effects.

Such an experiment would test a specified quantum preparation and
measurement model. It would NOT test a new elementary particle of
time or the origin of the universe. A physical proper-time clock
model would additionally require a Hamiltonian, clock coupling,
worldline specification, uncertainty budget, and independent
instrumentation. A photonic barrier hypothesis would require a
separate operational definition of its material or field behavior.

12. LIMITATIONS AND OPEN PROBLEMS

L1. The present qutrit state curve is an ideal mathematical model;
    it is not derived from a full photonic device Hamiltonian.
L2. The exact encoder works on a specified rational input domain;
    symbolic square roots and logarithms are not always evaluated.
L3. The exact 1,318-byte research script does not implement a
    general-purpose Brainfuck-8 interpreter, despite belonging to a
    project whose canonical machine uses such an interpreter.
L4. The original 1,934-byte script uses Python floating-point
    functions and cannot certify arbitrary numerical precision.
L5. Endpoint Fisher values require care: zero-probability events,
    parameter identifiability and regularity conditions matter.
L6. Stirling asymptotics and normalization do not eliminate
    curvature blow-ups or prove spacetime geodesic completeness.
L7. There is no demonstrated chronon, physical photonic singularity,
    universally protective photonized barrier, physical immortality,
    or oracle deciding halting on unrestricted inputs.
L8. General effective detection and removal of every mathematical
    singularity is not claimed; proposed algorithms must specify
    their function classes and accepted proof certificates.

Open tasks include verified transcendental interval arithmetic,
formal proofs in a proof assistant, robust parameter identifiability
analysis, device-level qutrit modeling, and a falsifiable optical
measurement protocol with explicit error budgets.

13. CONCLUSION

A simple three-outcome statistical model provides a rigorous example
in which Fisher information diverges in one coordinate but becomes
constant in another, while the associated pure quantum state extends
smoothly. Stirling asymptotics connect the same family to exact
combinatorial counting limits. Rational symbolic arithmetic and
reversible Godel encoding supply a precise, reproducible layer for
part of the mathematical description. These are useful components of
a proposed UCP-based verification program, provided its scope remains
explicit. None of them, separately or jointly, establishes a
physical cosmological singularity, a new time particle, or a
complete quantum-gravitational theory.
